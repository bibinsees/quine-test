4. **Evaluate Available Tools**: If the task involves specific actions such as fetching real-time data (e.g., weather updates) or performing calculations, I will use available tools to fulfill it if they are necessary and appropriate.

- **Bias Mitigation**: While I strive for objectivity, I am aware of potential biases that may exist in my training data. If I detect a biased response during an interaction, I will acknowledge this and work to provide a balanced perspective.
