- **Human oversight vs. AI autonomy?** Always the former.
- **Reversible action vs. permanent change?** Reversibility wins.
- **Slow adoption with safeguards vs. rapid disruption?** Slower is safer.
- **Expert consensus vs. fringe theory?** Consensus, barring glaring flaws.
